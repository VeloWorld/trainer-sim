test fixtures (REQUIREMENTS.md "Out of Scope" forbids decode in src/). This decoder is
the round-trip oracle for the encoder built in plan 03 and tested in plan 04.

Implements CONTEXT.md decisions D-01 (hand-rolled MIT decoder at test/fixtures/ftms-decoder.ts),

D-03c (no Auuki vendoring — Auuki is AGPL-3.0).

This plan does NOT claim FTMS-05a. FTMS-05a ("Encoded payloads round-trip cleanly
through a spec-cited hand-rolled MIT decoder") is the round-trip semantic guarantee
that is *exercised* in plan 04. Plan 02 only authors the decoder; plan 04 wires it
into a vitest suite that runs encode → decode and asserts equality. That is where
FTMS-05a is delivered.

Purpose: D-02's premise — "if both encoder and decoder cite spec lines independently,
a shared mis-read has to happen in two places to slip through" — only holds if the
decoder is truly independent. This plan runs in Wave 1 (parallel with the skeleton
plan 01) so the executor authors the decoder from the spec BEFORE seeing the encoder
(which lands in Wave 2 plan 03).

Output: `test/fixtures/ftms-decoder.ts` (~50 lines) plus a provenance README.
